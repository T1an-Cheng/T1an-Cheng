# Hi, I'm Tian Cheng 👋

I am a Research Assistant with the [Trustworthy Machine Learning and Reasoning (TMLR) Group](https://github.com/tmlr-group) at Hong Kong Baptist University, advised by [Prof. Bo Han](https://bhanml.github.io/).

I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from Huazhong University of Science and Technology. My research interests center on agentic reinforcement learning and trustworthy reasoning with foundation models. I enjoy building learning systems that bring together language models, tools, and verifiable feedback.

## Featured project

### [AlphaApollo](https://github.com/tmlr-group/AlphaApollo)

AlphaApollo is a system for deep agentic reasoning that integrates foundation models with professional tools and supports agentic learning and self-evolution.
